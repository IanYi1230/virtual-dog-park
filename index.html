Dog Encyclopedia.

Three lovely breeds. A great place to start.

01 — 03 ↘
01 the curious explorer ✧

Beagle

↗

A little nose for big adventures.

Size
Small to medium, with a sturdy build.
Personality
Curious, cheerful, and friendly. Loves following interesting smells.
Everyday care
Enjoy daily walks and sniffing games. Brush the short coat weekly and keep outdoor play in a secure area.
● Always exploring
02 the sunshine friend ✳

Golden Retriever

↗

A big heart with a very waggy tail.

Size
Large, with a strong, active body.
Personality
Affectionate, playful, and eager to learn. Often enjoys being around people.
Everyday care
Make time for daily exercise and play. Brush the thick coat several times a week to manage loose hair.
● Everybody’s buddy
03 the clever companion ✦

Poodle

↗

Curly coat. Bright mind. Lots of charm.

Size
Varies: Toy, Miniature, and Standard sizes.
Personality
Smart, lively, and attentive. Enjoys learning tricks and solving puzzles.
Everyday care
Offer daily walks and brain games. Brush the curly coat often and arrange regular grooming to prevent tangles.
● Quick to learn
